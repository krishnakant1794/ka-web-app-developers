## 📝 Tips for Adding Content

1. **Projects:**
   - Add at least 3-5 projects to showcase your work
   - Mix of Client Projects and Personal Projects
   - Include different types: Website, App, Full Stack
   - Add client feedback for Client Projects

2. **Pricing:**
   - Add all pricing plans (3 Website + 2 App plans recommended)
   - Mark the most popular plan with "Mark as Popular" checkbox
   - Be clear about what's included in each plan

3. **Testimonials:**
   - Add 4-6 testimonials for credibility
   - Mix different project types
   - Use 4-5 star ratings
   - Keep feedback authentic and specific

4. **Features & Tech Stack:**
   - List one item per line
   - Be specific about technologies
   - Don't over-promise in features
